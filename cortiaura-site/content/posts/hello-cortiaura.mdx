CortiAura is a UK neurotechnology company developing a non-invasive wearable built around the gut–brain connection. This blog will share clear, referenced explanations of the science, alongside updates on our progress.

Our principles:

- **Evidence-led:** guided by research, with transparency and rigour.
- **Safety first:** built to recognised medical device safety standards from the first prototype.
- **Non-invasive:** working with the body from the outside, with no implants and no drugs.
